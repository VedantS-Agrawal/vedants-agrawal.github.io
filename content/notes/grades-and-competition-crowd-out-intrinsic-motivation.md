Competition and grading are external motivators, and they reduce the drive that's intrinsic. When an early sense of security, autonomy, social support, and ability is threatened and made contingent on external rewards, a person has to work *against* their intrinsically motivating forces.

My ideal state is being entirely intrinsically motivated, while still having the markers of success in the society we live in.

Someone had to design the learning systems we're put through; they don't arise naturally. Would I have been taught how to naturally retain and remember, or would it always have been a struggle? And in my own classes I find it frankly ridiculous that we're told to learn in these ways instead of by ourselves — online, or reading to learn.
