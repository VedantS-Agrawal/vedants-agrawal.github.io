Reading back my own writing, I fail to be concise and add filler. Even some of the "ands" are just a wish to be verbose. State what I feel, without adding justifications and reasons for it.

To what extent have I prioritized complex sentences over clear ideas? Cut the unnecessary, verbose phrasing into authoritative, direct statements. Hard-to-read writing frustrates readers; it's only accessible to people who already believe you have something to say, never to someone coming to your words for the first time.

Clarity in writing comes from clarity in thought — the two are inextricably linked. Avoiding that work lands you in pseudo-intellectual territory: knowledge and phrases that carry depth only at first glance.

Being a writer isn't just having ideas; it's writing about them well — using the full extent of the language while minimizing complexity. Getting words onto the page is easy for me now. Going back over them and conveying them clearly still escapes me. (*On Writing Well* helped: much of what passes for grammar is really style.)
