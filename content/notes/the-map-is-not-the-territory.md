A map of reality isn't reality — maps are imperfect.

We need abstractions: descriptions, theories, models. But every map has three problems:
- It can be *incorrect* without our realizing it.
- It's a *reduction* of the real thing, so it loses fidelity.
- It needs *interpretation*, which can introduce major errors.
