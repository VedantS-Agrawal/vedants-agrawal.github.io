A paper idea from early 2024, set as a six-month goal: how cardiometabolic risk factors cluster, and the role sleep plays. Got as far as a framework.

Methods I was learning for it:

- [Principal Component Analysis](/notes/principal-component-analysis) and factor analysis — collapse many correlated risk factors into a few underlying ones.
- <span class="link-private" title="Not public yet">An Intuitive Introduction to Multinomial Logit</span> — the plan was to compare multinomial logit with factor analysis for cardiovascular risk assessment.

Anchor paper: Tsai et al. (2020), *Factor analysis for the clustering of cardiometabolic risk factors and sedentary behavior*. Five factors — adiposity, lipids, blood pressure, glucose, sedentary behavior — explained 69.4% of the variance; hsCRP clustered with adiposity, not with the other cardiometabolic factors.

Part of a larger goal that year to learn machine learning and write a paper by year's end.
