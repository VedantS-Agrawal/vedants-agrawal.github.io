I hurry through life. I want things done fast, I want "better" ways of doing things — and I often can't find the patience and calm to hear a friend out, which is the truest display of empathy.

What's the belief underneath? Anything that wants the world to unfold in a way it doesn't. Hurry, at the core. There's no room for reflection during hurry. No room for kindness. No room for deep thought — hurry is the antithesis of deep. We can't keep a sense of calm and patience and understanding while constantly in the throes of hurry.
