Life is attention. What we pay attention to sets the direction of our lives and defines our lived experience of them.

To focus deeper, block out irrelevant stimuli and train attention to stay rooted on its object — meditation in its forms (loving-kindness, open monitoring, focused attention) is the scientifically supported way.

The mind will focus on what it desires, but I'm a function of the *awareness* of the mind; I can choose the salient cues and hone in on them. I keep forgetting I'm responsible for my mental state: I can move attention toward what fulfills me, which is usually in the outside world. Caught up in thought, I keep feeding the same emotional cycle.

Think of the brain as an engine and consciousness as the steering wheel: wherever you direct attention, the brain follows. The trouble starts when you steer toward an unsolvable problem. (From <span class="link-private" title="Not public yet">Hyperfocus</span>.)
