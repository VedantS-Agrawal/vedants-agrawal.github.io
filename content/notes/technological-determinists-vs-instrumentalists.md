- **Determinists**: the technologies we use change how we think, and we have little to no control over how.
- **Instrumentalists**: we control technology; it is entirely subservient to the wishes of its user.
 

[The Shallows](/library/the-shallows-what-the-internet-is-doing-to-our-brains) sides with the determinists — the medium, not the content, changes our patterns of perception. [Time-Keeping](/notes/time-keeping) is the example. Technology creates another way of interacting with the world and offloading some previously necessary capabilities to open a new avenue of discovery and innovation. Socrates and Plato once held fears of writing as removing the didactic memory of old, where orators could deliver novels of prose and mythology by memory and the process of dialogue with others were how great ideas were formed.
