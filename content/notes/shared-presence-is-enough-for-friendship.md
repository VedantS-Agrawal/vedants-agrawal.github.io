Social interactions don't need to signal complete understanding, reach a shared understanding of a topic, or even touch our baseline emotions. They can be as simple as shared presence — someone in the room, a repeated interaction, a warm wave from a regular you see.

Those are the interactions that activate the prosocial networks of the brain and the positive response that comes with them. In what way am I failing to take advantage of them?
