There's a significant problem in how our brains — shaped first for Paleolithic life, then for Neolithic life in small tribes, and later for organizing around cultural myths into larger social structures — interact with the technology we're exposed to now.

The modes of thinking cultivated by those older ways of living collide with modern tech, causing tension, conflict, and misaligned aims and incentives.
