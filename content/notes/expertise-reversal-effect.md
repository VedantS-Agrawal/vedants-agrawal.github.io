Instruction that helps learners build long-term schemas works best for novices, who have no such structures to lean on. For experts, less guidance often beats well-guided instruction.

Same reason the [Worked Example Effect](/notes/worked-example-effect) fades with prior knowledge: once a problem-solving schema exists, giving it again is redundant.
