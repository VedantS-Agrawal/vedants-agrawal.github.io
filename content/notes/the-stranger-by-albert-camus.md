I feel disoriented after reading this book -- the presentation of a way of life in which things that are held sacred by societal customs are ignored by a protagonists whose way of life is cherished by himself towards the end of the book is unsettling. What have I for a moral compass if not those things that the society in which I live in tells me is right or wrong? This presentation makes me question whether the emotions I wear are my own or another's.
