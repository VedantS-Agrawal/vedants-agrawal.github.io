Productivity should only be defined by how far the desired activity got accomplished. It's tied to an intentional goal, or the intentional process of completing something — it doesn't mean busy.

As long as I'm acting intentionally toward my goals, that's productive. Whatever pulls me away from what I want to accomplish is distraction. Written while reading <span class="link-private" title="Not public yet">Psycho-Cybernetics</span>: pick something to aim the goal-seeking mechanism at — finishing work, the gym, reading, being present with people.
