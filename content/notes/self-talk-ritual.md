From Mark Queppet's *The Reboot Regimen*. The point: improve the quality of internal discussion so the rational mind can interpret emotional responses and steer them toward actions that help.

1. **Ground.** Tension in the body tracks negative emotion and short-circuits relaxed, rational thinking. A few deep breaths, a body scan, engage the senses.
2. **Connect.** Bring awareness to the emotional center and observe whatever's there, without judgment. You can't change what's there, only notice it.
3. **Translate.** Put the felt sense into a sentence. Don't filter — we think a lot of nonsense; we aren't meant to take every thought seriously.
4. **Respond** — with truth, as a friend, as a leader. Accept the desire, then state honestly what acting on it leads to, and whether it moves you toward your best life.

You have to feed these truths to yourself in the moment.
