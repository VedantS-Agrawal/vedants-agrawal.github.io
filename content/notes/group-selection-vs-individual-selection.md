Groups are a collection of individuals each acting to optimize their own well-being. That includes supposed altruism — we're altruistic when there's a benefit likely to pay off over time.

Assuming a group will be inherently altruistic ignores drift and mutation. In the real world a selfish mutant would be advantaged and spread, exploiting everyone else's altruism.

- **Signaling theory.** Even acts with no possible payback signal to the community who is trustworthy and generous.
- **Tit for tat.** Further reading: *The Selfish Gene*.
