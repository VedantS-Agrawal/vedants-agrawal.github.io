Being in the present avoids the short-circuiting of self-conscious thought. Self-conscious thoughts turn into rumination, and attention gets fed into them, taking resources from problem-solving. Like thinking about whether you'll make the shot while you're shooting — it takes you out of flow.

That's why spontaneous speaking is hard: the self-conscious circuits switch on.

Ways to cultivate it:
- Reframe challenging questions as the biggest opportunities. Someone challenging you is engaged — they want to understand.
- Shoot for average, fail cheerfully.
- Be obvious; that's where your truest voice is.
- Be uncomfortable enough to plug in — get lost on purpose and see what you find.
