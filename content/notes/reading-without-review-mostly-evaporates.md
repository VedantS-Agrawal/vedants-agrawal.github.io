There's no purpose to reading educational material without making review material for it. Otherwise I won't make progress in my understanding.

My retention of a text depends on how long it's been since I last read it and how many days in a row I read and reviewed it — spaced repetition and consistency, plainly. Writing and speaking about what I learned, then reviewing and speaking about it again, would help too.

What the review material should look like:
1. Cards I can review in a year and still know what source they came from and what concept they mean.
2. Atomic — the smallest possible unit. The organization comes from integrating those atoms into my own schema.

Two years later in medical school, same lesson from Anki: information under regular spaced repetition is closer at hand than anything recalled after long gaps.
