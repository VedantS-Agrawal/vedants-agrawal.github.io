After vigorous cardio I feel much more comfortable in my own mind — not racing, grounded and stable.

The axiom to remember: whenever I feel depressed, purposeless, or anxious, work out. Run, walk, get the heart rate up, bring the body to physical exhaustion. Once I'm no longer brimming with energy for the myriad ideas my mind throws at me, I can be selective and pick the ones worth pursuing.
