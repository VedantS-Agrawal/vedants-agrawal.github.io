An approach to restoring attention. Environments with stimuli that modestly engage *involuntary* attention let *directed* attention recover.

Attention splits into bottom-up (involuntary) and top-down (voluntary). Directed attention is the part of top-down attention that has to suppress distracting stimulation — and it's the part that tires.

The study ([The Cognitive Benefits of Interacting with Nature](/library/the-cognitive-benefits-of-interacting-with-nature)): people walked for an hour in either an urban or a natural setting. The nature walkers did better on the backward digit-span test and on an Attention Network Task, and their mood improved (though mood wasn't what explained the attention gains).

- **Backward digit span** — listen to sequences of 3–9 digits, repeat them backwards. Measures directed-attention performance.
- **PANAS** (Positive and Negative Affect Schedule) — rate mood adjectives 1–5 for how well they describe you right now.
