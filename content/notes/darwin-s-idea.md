Natural selection: organisms vary, the variants that suit the needs of their environment are selected for, and the population changes.

When Darwin proposed it, the unit of heredity — the gene — hadn't been identified. Mendel's work would come out years later and take much longer to reach mainstream science. Groundbreaking as the idea was, it took a lot of further work to show it was right and displace the paradigms of the time.

See [Galapagos Finch](/notes/galapagos-finch), and the idea it displaced: [Law of Use and Disuse](/notes/law-of-use-and-disuse).
