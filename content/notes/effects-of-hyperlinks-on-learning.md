Book readers show activity in regions for language, memory, and visual processing. Net users show extensive activity in regions for decision-making and problem-solving — because every link is a decision: click or don't.

Those decisions take up room in working memory, a cognitive tax on moving information into the richer schemas of long-term memory. See [Cognitive Load](/notes/cognitive-load).

- Long-form readers go line by line; web readers scan in an "F" pattern. Only about 18% of an article's information gets read.
- Asked to compare two contrasting texts, people who read them linearly did better than people who switched back and forth.
