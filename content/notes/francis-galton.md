English polymath, another grandson of Erasmus Darwin, and the first to apply statistics to the inheritance of intelligence. No IQ tests existed yet, so he used other measures of competence — ability, and "reputation" for contributing to society.

Came up with correlation, championed regression to the mean, and devised measures like the median and quartiles.

On [Nature vs Nurture](/notes/nature-vs-nurture) he concluded that nature prevails enormously. He wasn't the first to suggest a mechanism of heredity for behavior, but he was the first to document it extensively. He also originated eugenics — which fed ideas of racial purity (<span class="link-private" title="Not public yet">Fatal Invention</span>).
