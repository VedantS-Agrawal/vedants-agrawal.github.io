Two perspectives on a culture, named after phonetic vs phonemic:

- **Etic** (from phonetic — the sounds) — the outsider's objective evaluation of a culture's activities or customs.
- **Emic** (from phonemic — the meaning) — the insider's perspective on what those activities and conditions mean.

A related point from the same class: we all have the capacity for the same primary emotions across cultures, but how they're interpreted and understood depends on the cultural context we live in.
