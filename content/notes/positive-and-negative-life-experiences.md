**Positive life experiences** cluster in young adulthood (20–45), with the adoption of more social roles — living alone, a job, new friends, partners, other responsibilities. Over that period, neuroticism goes down.

How are we defining a positive life experience, though, and why would taking on more roles reduce neuroticism? Is autonomy the driving factor in the growth of the individual? What else could explain it?

**Negative life experiences** are spread more evenly across the lifespan — they're deviations from life's normal progress — though they still trend upward over time.
