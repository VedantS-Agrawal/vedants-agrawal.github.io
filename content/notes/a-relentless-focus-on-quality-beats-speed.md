I've been reaching for quick, easier solutions in the name of progress and efficiency — partly because I feel the need to finish faster and be more productive.

With a relentless focus on quality, I make fewer mistakes, which limits the extra work I have to do in the first place, and the work itself is higher quality and higher impact. The interesting projects in front of me need the time and energy, plus that focus on quality, to develop the expertise they call for.
