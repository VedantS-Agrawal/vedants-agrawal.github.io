The resistance you feel when you know there's something you can do and you're refusing to do it — the friction my brain generates whenever I want to start an activity.

One way through it: make a list of painful activities and make yourself do them. Steepen and deepen the trough of amotivation and pain:
- cold shower or ice bath
- a meditation session
- a set number of pushups, squats, situps
- learning something difficult
