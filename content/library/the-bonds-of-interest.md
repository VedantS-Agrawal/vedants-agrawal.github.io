Read for a Nobel Prizes in Literature class. My essay: <span class="link-private" title="Not public yet">Weakness and manipulation in The Bonds of Interest</span>.

Jacinto Benavente's 1907 comedy borrows its masks from the commedia dell'arte: two penniless adventurers, the romantic Leandro and his scheming servant Crispín, arrive in a city and con their way into society by pretending to be rich. Crispín pulls every string — he works out what each person wants and sells it back to them. Benavente won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1922.

The dry joke is in the title: everyone is held together by interest, and love wins only once the debts are settled.
